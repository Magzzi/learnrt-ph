# M1: Offline core app

Status: draft for review. Terms follow [GLOSSARY.md](../../GLOSSARY.md). Milestone outcome from [PRD §12](../PRD.md#12-milestones): *Expo app with schematic map, Station detail, fares, Nearest Station, learn cards, English and Filipino.*

## Why

M1 is the first thing a rider can hold. It has to be useful with no signal and no account (PRD §14, cold start), because Guided Ride (M2) and the live layer (M3) build on it.

## User stories

1. As a first-time rider, I open the app and see a map of every Line, so I understand how the network fits together before I go.
2. As a first-time rider, I tap a Station and see its Line, the Stations either side, any Transfer to another Line, and landmarks nearby.
3. As a rider, I pick an origin and destination on one Line, a ticket type and whether I get a Discounted Fare, and see the Fare.
4. As a rider standing somewhere in the city, I tap "Nearest Station" and see the three closest Stations with their distances, even offline.
5. As a curious rider, I read about each Line, and see Coming Soon cards for Lines that are not open yet.
6. As a Filipino-speaking rider, I use the whole app in Filipino; Station names stay official.
7. As any rider, I can see that the data is not yet verified and that the app is unofficial, so I know how far to trust it.

## Acceptance criteria

### App shell
- `apps/mobile`: Expo SDK 58, Expo Router, TypeScript, NativeWind. It imports `@learnrt/core` from the workspace.
- Four tabs: **Map**, **Fares**, **Nearest**, **Learn**. About is reached from Learn.
- Dark theme taken from the website (`#0a1a1a` base, `#0d2222` panels, `#1a2e2e` raised, emerald accent, yellow-500 primary button).
- Works in airplane mode, except for opening the OpenStreetMap attribution link.
- `npx expo export` builds Android and iOS bundles; `npx expo-doctor` passes.

### Data (in `packages/core`)
- Station coordinates come from OpenStreetMap (approved source for D7 and D8). Each Station records its OSM element id; the dataset carries ODbL attribution. LRT-2 Santolan moves to its OSM position (D7 fix).
- MRT-7 Stations missing from OSM keep their current coordinates and are marked as not from OSM.
- Landmarks are migrated from the website's landmark page into core, keyed by Station id, unchanged and unverified (new D14).
- Station display names change to the approved OSM spellings in [station-names-proposal.md](../station-names-proposal.md) (D6). Ids never change; a test pins the full id list so a rename can only touch display names.
- Every rider-facing dataset (Stations, Fares, Transfers, landmarks) reports `lastVerified`. While it is `null`, the app shows an **Unverified** label next to that data.

### Map (F1)
- A schematic diagram of LRT-1, LRT-2 and MRT-3, drawn with react-native-svg from core data (no map tiles). Transfers are drawn between their Stations. MRT-7 is drawn dashed and labelled Coming Soon.
- Pan and pinch-zoom. Tapping a Station opens Station detail. Each Station has an accessible label ("Doroteo Jose, LRT-1").

### Station detail (F2, offline part)
- Shows name, Line, Line Status, and the neighbouring Stations (both tappable).
- Shows any Transfer: the other Station and Line, plus "Walking directions not available yet" (D10). Labelled Unverified.
- Shows nearby landmarks (Unverified).
- Entrances, exits, platforms per direction and elevators show "Not available yet" (no data exists: new D15). Rider Reports and Advisories are not shown (M3, M4).
- Has a "Fares from here" action that opens Fares with this Station as origin.

### Fares (F3)
- Line, origin, destination, ticket (Single Journey Ticket or Stored Value Ticket), Discounted Fare on/off. The result comes from `getFare` and is labelled Unverified.
- When there is no Fare (for example the same Station on a Single Journey Ticket), the screen says so instead of showing ₱0.
- Origin and destination are on one Line; cross-Line Fares are M2.

### Nearest Station (F4)
- Asks for foreground location only, with a plain explanation first.
- Shows the three nearest Stations on operational Lines, with distance (metres under 1 km, else km to one decimal). Each opens Station detail.
- Permission denied or location unavailable: explains why and offers the Map instead. No geocoding and no network calls.

### Learn (F10)
- One card per operational Line: name, Stations in order, and areas served, as on the website's explore page (Unverified).
- Coming Soon cards for MRT-7, Metro Manila Subway, NSCR and PNR, as in PRD §6. A card shows a Station list only where the repo has one.
- First-timer tips: short, general how-to copy (buying a ticket, the security check, tapping in and out, waiting behind the platform line). No Fares, hours or headways in the copy. The owner reviews it before launch.
- About: "Unofficial; not affiliated with LRMC, LRTA or DOTr" (PRD §11), "© OpenStreetMap contributors" with ODbL, and what Unverified means.

### Language (F13)
- English and Filipino via i18next and expo-localization. Every Filipino string is marked "needs review" in the translation file until the owner and a native speaker review it (a launch gate, PRD §11). The default follows the device language; there is a manual switch in About.
- Every rider-facing string goes through i18n. A test fails if a key is missing in either language. Station and Line names are never translated.

## Edge cases
- The device language is neither English nor Filipino: use English.
- The location fix is far from Metro Manila: still show the three nearest, with large distances. No cut-off.
- The same Station is picked as origin and destination: see Fares above.
- Same-named Stations (two Santolan, two Cubao): always shown with their Line.
- Small screens and large font sizes: no text clipped on a 360 dp-wide screen at 1.3× font scale.

## Out of scope
- Guided Ride, cross-Line routing and Fares, geofences, notifications (M2).
- Street-level map with MapLibre and OSM tiles (F1 second half): last ticket of M2 (owner, 2026-10-28).
- Supabase, accounts, Rider Reports, Advisories, the SQLite snapshot and sync (M3, M4). In M1, data is bundled through `@learnrt/core`.
- Operating hours, open/closed status, Headway Estimate (D11, D12).
- EAS Build, store builds, Sentry, analytics (M6).
