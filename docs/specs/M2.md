# M2: Guided Ride

Status: draft for review. Terms follow [GLOSSARY.md](../../GLOSSARY.md). Milestone outcome from [PRD §12](../PRD.md#12-milestones): *Cross-Line route planning, step screens, background geofences, notifications, manual fallback; street-level map (F1) as the last, cuttable ticket.*

## Why

Guided Ride is differentiator 1 (PRD §4): a first-time rider should be able to ride from any Station to any other, across Lines, without asking anyone, with the phone in their pocket. It must work offline and without an account (PRD §8).

## User stories

1. As a first-time rider, I pick an origin and a destination Station, even on different Lines, and see the route: each Line I ride, how many stops, where I Transfer, and the Fare for each Line.
2. As a first-time rider, I start a Guided Ride and see one step at a time: how to pay, which direction to take, how many stops, where and how to Transfer, when to get off.
3. As a rider with the phone locked in my pocket, I get a notification when I am one Station away from where I get off, and when I arrive.
4. As a rider on Android, an ongoing notification shows how many stops are left.
5. As a rider whose GPS is wrong or missing, I tap "I'm at ___" to tell the app where I am, and the steps follow.
6. As a rider, I end a Guided Ride at any time; if the app closes, I can resume the ride when I reopen it.
7. As a rider, I see the Fare between any two Stations on the Fares tab, including across Lines.
8. (Cuttable) As a rider walking to a Station, I see it on a street map with my position.

## Acceptance criteria

### Route planning (core, F5)
- `planRoute(origin, destination)` in `packages/core` returns a route made of legs (one Line each, Stations in riding order, number of stops, direction) joined by Transfers. It uses only operational Lines and the Transfers in core.
- **Route choice (decision D-M2-1, owner):** proposed default: the route with the fewest Transfers; if another route has fewer stops, it is offered as an alternative ("Fewer stops, one more Transfer"). No travel times are invented: none are known (D10, D12).
- A leg's direction is named by the last Station of the Line in the direction of travel, from core's Line order ("towards Dr. Santos"). The operators' own direction wording is unverified (new D21).
- Origin equal to destination: no route; the screen says so.
- Origin and destination at the two ends of one Transfer (for example Doroteo Jose and Recto): a route with no train leg, only the walk.

### Route Fare (core, F3 and F5)
- The route Fare is one Fare per leg from `getFare`, for the chosen ticket and Discounted Fare setting, plus the total. If any leg has no Fare, the total is shown as unknown, never as a partial sum.
- Labelled Unverified while `fareData.lastVerified` is null.

### Guided Ride steps (core)
- `rideSteps(route, ticket)` turns a route into steps, in order:
  - **Enter** the origin Station. Which entrance: "Not available yet" (D15).
  - **Pay** for this Line: "Buy a Single Journey Ticket (₱N)" or "Tap your Stored Value Ticket (₱N)", then tap in at the gate.
  - **Board** the train towards the direction Station. Which platform: "Not available yet" (D15).
  - **Ride** N stops to the Station where you get off. This step tracks the current Station.
  - **Transfer** (per Transfer): get off, tap out, walk to the other Station and Line. "Walking directions not available yet" (D10). Then Pay and Board again for the next Line.
  - **Arrive**: get off at the destination and tap out.
- Steps hold Station ids and numbers only; the app renders the text through i18n in English and Filipino.

### Ride progress (core)
- A pure state machine: `start(route)`, `arrivedAt(stationId)` (from a geofence), `imAt(stationId)` (manual), `end()`.
- Arriving at a Station later on the route jumps forward to it (a missed geofence is skipped). Arriving at a Station behind the current position, or not on the route, does not change progress from GPS.
- "I'm at ___" may move progress forward or back, to any Station on the route.
- Progress reports: current step, current Station, stops left on this leg and on the whole ride, and which alert is due: "get off at the next Station" (one Station before a Transfer or the destination) and "get off now" (at that Station). Each alert fires at most once per Station.
- Arriving at the destination completes the ride.

### Geofences (core and app)
- Core picks the regions: the route's Stations after the current one, at most 20 at a time (iOS limit), re-registered as the ride advances. Radius is 150 m, reduced to half the distance to the nearest other Station when that is smaller, so two regions never overlap. The radius is a tuning setting, not Station data.
- The app registers them with `expo-location` geofencing and an `expo-task-manager` task, so progress continues with the screen locked.

### Screens (app)
- A fifth tab, **Ride**: origin and destination pickers (any operational Station, shown with its Line), ticket and Discounted Fare, the route summary with legs, Transfers, stops and per-leg Fares (Unverified), the alternative when there is one, and **Start Guided Ride**.
- Station detail gains "Ride from here" and "Ride to here".
- The ride screen shows the current step, the Stations ahead on this leg, stops left, an "I'm at ___" picker limited to the route's Stations, and **End ride**.
- The active ride is saved on the device. Reopening the app during a ride returns to the ride screen.
- Fares tab: origin and destination may be on different Lines; it shows the per-leg Fares and the total from the route (closes M1's "cross-Line Fares are M2").

### Permissions and notifications (app)
- Before asking for background location, a plain explanation screen: why ("so the app can tell you when to get off while your phone is locked"), that only Station areas on the route are watched, and that nothing leaves the phone. Then foreground, then background ("Allow all the time") permission.
- If background location or notifications are refused, Guided Ride still works with the app open and with "I'm at ___"; the ride screen says alerts are off and links to settings.
- Notifications (local only, no push): "Get off at the next Station: ___", "Get off now: ___", and at a Transfer "Walk to ___ (Line)". One Android channel, "Guided Ride".
- Android: an ongoing (non-swipeable) notification shows "N stops to ___" and is updated as the ride advances; it is removed when the ride ends.
- iOS: notifications as above. Live Activity is out of scope (decision D-M2-3).

### Development build
- M2 needs a development build: on Android, Expo Go does not run `expo-task-manager`, so geofences cannot work there. The app moves from Expo Go to a development build with `expo-dev-client` (decision D-M2-2: built locally, or in EAS).
- `npx expo export` for Android and iOS still succeeds; `npx expo-doctor` passes.

### Street-level map (F1, last ticket, cuttable)
- A street map screen from Station detail: the Station, the rider's position, and the Station's entrances when the data exists. MapLibre React Native.
- Needs owner decisions before it starts: the tile source (D-M2-4) and whether OSM entrance nodes are an approved source (D-M2-5). If M2 runs long, this ticket moves to a later milestone.

### Language
- Every new rider-facing string is in English and Filipino; every Filipino string is marked "needs review". Station and Line names are not translated.

## Edge cases
- Origin and destination on the same Line: one leg, no Transfer.
- Origin and destination are the same Station: no route.
- Two Lines can be joined two ways (LRT-1, LRT-2 and MRT-3 form a loop through Doroteo Jose–Recto, EDSA–Taft Avenue and Cubao): see route choice above.
- A Station with the same name on two Lines (Santolan, Cubao): always shown with its Line, picked by id.
- Two Stations of a Transfer are close together: geofence radius shrinks so they do not overlap.
- GPS jumps to a Station behind or off the route: progress ignores it.
- GPS never fires (no fix, emulator, tunnel-like conditions): "I'm at ___" moves the ride.
- The app is killed during a ride: Android does not restart it on a geofence event (Expo docs). Alerts stop until the rider reopens the app, which resumes the ride. The ride screen tells the rider to keep the app in recent apps.
- Permissions revoked mid-ride: the ride continues in the foreground and says alerts are off.
- A leg has no Fare: total shown as unknown.
- Rides including LRT-1 Cavite extension Stations: allowed, as in core today; whether each is open is unverified (D9).
- Large font and 360 dp screens: no clipped text, as in M1.

## Out of scope
- Travel time, arrival time or Headway Estimate (D12, F9).
- iOS Live Activity, home-screen widgets (M6 or later; D-M2-3).
- Saved Commutes (M6), Rider Reports and Segment Timings during a ride (M3, M5).
- Walking directions to or between Stations (no data, no routing service).
- Push notifications or any server (M3+).
- iOS device verification: there is no Mac and no Apple Developer account yet.

## Owner decisions needed before tickets start
- **D-M2-1 Route choice:** fewest Transfers by default, fewer-stops route as an alternative (proposed). Alternatives: always fewest stops; or fewest stops with each Transfer counted as a fixed number of stops (that number would be a guess).
- **D-M2-2 Development build:** build locally with `npx expo run:android` (proposed; needs JDK 17 and Android build tools, about 5–6 GB of downloads, no accounts) or in the cloud with EAS (needs an Expo account, which only the owner can create).
- **D-M2-3 iOS Live Activity:** defer to M6, when an Apple Developer account exists (proposed), since it cannot be built or tested here.
- **D-M2-4 Street map tiles:** OpenStreetMap's own tile servers are not meant for app traffic. Options: OpenFreeMap (free, no account), or a provider with an account (MapTiler, Stadia Maps; free tiers, owner account).
- **D-M2-5 Entrances from OSM:** approve OSM entrance nodes as a source for the street map (they would stay Unverified), or show no entrances.

1. `pnpm test`: core tests for route planning, route Fare, steps, progress and geofence regions; mobile tests; i18n key test.
2. `pnpm typecheck` and `npx expo-doctor` pass; `npx expo export --platform android --platform ios` succeeds; `pnpm build:web` still succeeds.

Recorded in a demo checklist with pass or fail per step.
5. Same playback in airplane mode, and once in Filipino.

7. Kill the app mid-ride, reopen it: the ride resumes.
8. iOS: bundle export only. Device behaviour stays unverified until an Apple account exists.
