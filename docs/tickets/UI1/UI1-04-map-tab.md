# UI1-04: Map tab: welcome card, map card and controls

Spec: [UI1](../../specs/UI1.md) · Depends on: UI1-02

## What
Welcome card with an original SVG illustration; the Line map in a card with title and subtitle, "Find a station" (accent- and case-insensitive search, opens Station detail), zoom +/−, "show me", legend with Unverified, and the tip bar. Map colours for light and dark. Search matching is a pure, tested function.

## Acceptance criteria
- Pan, pinch and tap still work; zoom buttons zoom about the centre; "show me" centres on the rider or explains why it can't.
- "dona" finds Doña Carmen; no match says so.
