# M1-09: Learn cards and About

Spec: [M1](../../specs/M1.md) · Depends on: M1-04

## What
The Learn tab: one card per operational Line (Stations in order and areas served, from the website's explore page, Unverified), Coming Soon cards (MRT-7, Metro Manila Subway, NSCR, PNR; Station lists only where the repo has them), first-timer tips (general how-to only), and About (unofficial disclaimer, © OpenStreetMap contributors / ODbL with link, what Unverified means, language switch).

## Acceptance criteria
- The disclaimer text names LRMC, LRTA and DOTr, and no operator logos appear.
- The tips contain no Fares, hours or headways.
- The OSM attribution link opens openstreetmap.org/copyright.
