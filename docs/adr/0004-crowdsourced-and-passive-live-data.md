# Source live data from riders and curation because no operator feed exists

LRT-1, LRT-2 and MRT-3 publish no real-time train data. Live information comes from Rider Reports (signed-in, near the Station, confirmed or disputed by others), from Delay Signals computed from opt-in anonymous Segment Timings, from Headway Estimates, and from Advisories curated by an admin. Scraping operator social media was rejected as brittle and a possible Terms of Service problem. The accumulated report and timing data is also what makes the app hard to copy.
